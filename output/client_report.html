Key Observations

Active portfolio

The business is currently engaged with 151 strata managers across 655 distinct properties. Revenue in the FY26 to date totals $3.60M from 1,936 completed jobs, at an average of $2k per job.

Concentration risk

GK Strata Management Pty Ltd accounts for 24% of active-book revenue on its own. The top 3 clients together account for 58% and the top 5 for 69%. The business is heavily dependent on a small number of strata relationships.

Growth

O'Neill Strata Management is the strongest growth story, with FY26 revenue of $71k up 765% on FY25 ($8k). Also growing strongly: Jamesons (+276%), BFMS (+59%).

Clients to watch

Montano Strata has declined 99% from FY25 ($28k) to FY26 ($340). Other notable declines: John (-93%), StrataWide (-87%). Use the Client Spotlight to see which properties are driving these changes.

Property depth

The top 10 individual properties account for 13% of active revenue. The single highest-value property is 1-7 Gloucester Place, Kensington (Kensington) at $75k from 54 jobs, managed by Result Property Group.

Overall trajectory

On an annualised basis, FY26 revenue is tracking 21% growing versus FY25. FY26 data runs to 4 June 2026, covering 339 of 365 days of the financial year.
