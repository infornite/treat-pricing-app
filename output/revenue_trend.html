How to use this dashboard

Primary chart always shows Jul to Jun on the x-axis. Toggle FY24, FY25 and FY26 to overlay or compare years. Switch to FY change % to see each year as a percentage move on the one before it.

Drill-down — click any month bar to open the breakdown panel. It shows revenue by reactive system (sewer, hot water, etc.) and by quoted work type for each active FY, so you can see what drove a strong or weak month.

FY definition — FY24 = Jul 2023 to Jun 2024; FY25 = Jul 2024 to Jun 2025; FY26 = Jul 2025 to Jun 2026.

Revenue = Task Invoices Total Ex (ex-GST). Won quotes are counted once, on their completion date. Reactive = everything else.
